## 3rd time's the charm?
To anyone out there in the world wide web, welcome to my website and specifically my blog. I have been interested in the indie web since 2019, but had never been fully committed to having my own website. I spent a few years between 2019 and 2023 trying to get this off the ground, but life events hindered any progress. After being married almost 5 years and 2 kids later, I never appreciated how much free time I had, and that my reasons prior were excuses! Therefore I am restarting this website for the **3rd** time! 

In previous iterations I didn't have any goal in mind, but in my failures I have learned what I wish to achieve with this.

- I want to improve my writing
- I want to be active in the indie web
- I want my content to be my portfolio
- I want to learn more about myself

And I hope to make progress in these goals by committing to one task:
- Write **ONE** blog post per month

Let's see if this works, see you hopefully in 1 month's time.
