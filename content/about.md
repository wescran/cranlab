My name is Wesley Cranston and I am a software developer that currently works for [Psiphon](https://psiphon.ca/). I live in Toronto, Ontario, Canada with my amazing wife and 2 daughters.

I graduated from Queen's University in 2017 with a MASc in materials engineering. While working as an engineer, I spent my free time learning how to program with python. Programming led me to Linux which then began my interest in homelabbing. Thankfully I was able to turn this hobby into a career.

Besides my homelab, I love video games, movies, and spending time with my family. I am trying to read more, and rehab my knee so I can return to my favourite sport: squash. I am a passionate Leafs fan and hope to one day watch them lift the cup.
